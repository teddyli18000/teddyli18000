# Living Editorial Field — authored motion pass

The profile should feel like a person made it before it feels like an AI product page: warm paper, strong editorial type, restrained physical motion, real work, real contribution data, then a playful reveal.

## Hero

- Mineral paper/graphite canvas, EB Garamond/Georgia-style display serif, simple sans tagline.
- Right-side motion is a small family of translucent vellum ribbons. They breathe slowly and catch one soft moving sheen.
- No node network, routing trace, token, signal dot, constellation, glass blob, or pseudo-technical label.
- The animation is 64 frames over roughly 11.5 seconds; first and last frames coincide. Reduced-motion users receive the complete first-frame PNG.

## Work motion

- Millikan AI gets a literal measurement vignette: two reference plates and one measured oil drop moving between them.
- Side Quests gets a tiny app window taking an unnecessary detour over a mundane obstacle.
- These are punctuation, not product screenshots or cards.

## Live

- Data, not decoration, makes this section alive.
- Exactly three truthful values: contributions YTD, active public repositories, and upstream PRs.
- No status dot, pulse, glass dashboard card, fake monitoring label, or decorative animation.
- The daily Action updates the SVG, three curated upstream PRs, timestamp, and date-seeded footer. On API failure, keep the last-good snapshot.

## Palette

- Light canvas: `#F8F6F1`
- Dark canvas: `#121314`
- Coral: `#C06049`
- Sage: `#708774`
- Sand: `#B5966E`

The color should feel printed/material, never neon or tech-branded.
